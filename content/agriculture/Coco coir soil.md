Coconut coir is essentially 
the reuse of coconut waste 
to create a growing medium. 
It is biodegradable and 
perfect for microgreens. 

---

Given that the nutrients for microgreens come solely from the seed itself, water, and photosynthesis, the growing medium is not that important. 

This is thanks to the **endosperm reserves**. In the initial phase, no soil is needed for nutrients. 
The soil merely serves as a water delivery system and anchor.

Thanks to the aeration capacity of coconut coir (**macropores**), oxygen can circulate freely amongst the seeds, creating the perfect ecosystem for microgreens to grow. 

Coconut coir creates air channels that remain open, even when the medium is saturated with seeds or other materials. 

It has good water absorption and retention capacity. 
The moisture levels are just right for them.
