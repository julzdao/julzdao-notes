## Beyond the Lab

My work does not exist separately from the rest of my life.

I have practised Tai Chi for more than ten years and study the
philosophical traditions surrounding Taoism and Zen. I also
study chess, and play music with my guitar almost every day.

These practices often lead me back to the same questions that appear
in software and agriculture: How should a system respond to change?
What makes a structure resilient? How much can be removed before
something loses its essence?
