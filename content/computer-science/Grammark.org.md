Grammark is an 
open source tool 
which helps you 
improve your writing 
in a simple and effective way. 

---

Write your note. Then Paste it in [Grammark Main Page](https://grammark.org/#/). Click Submit. Then Go through each specific point to improve your writing. 

Going from Passive voice to Grammar Taps. 

Instead of letting AI or some other tool check your writing, this tool helps to be mindful about each word you use. Something slightly more analog than just "Please Improve my text". 

#### Grammark helps with:

- Academic style
- Passive voice
- Wordiness
- Nominalizations
- Transitions
- Sentence variety
- Run-on sentences
- Eggcorns
- Grammar traps

Check the [About Page](https://grammark.org/#/page/about) to understand somewhat more about the philosophy behind the project.
