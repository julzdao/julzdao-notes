Communication is the process 
of sending, receiving and 
exchanging information, 
ideas, thoughts or feelings 
between two agents.

---

Communication is simply the act of moving information from one place to another. 

In fact, the whole point of building computer software is about moving [[byte]]s from one point to another. That is to say writing code so that information flows without limitations. 

Alternatively, it is also a key element within a team. Being able to communicate ideas, concepts, problems well within a team is decisive. One may use [[Visual Thinking]] for creating diagrams, creating [[Small Pull Requests]] for being clear about each PR, or commenting in [[Jira Communication|JIRA]] for tracking and being transparent about the work.
