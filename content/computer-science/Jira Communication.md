---

Commenting on a **Jira** ticket isn’t about documenting every single step, but about keeping the information visible that others need to understand the status of the work.

A comment adds value particularly when a **blocker** arises, a technical decision changes, clarification is needed, or the ticket is ready for review or testing.

> [!tip]
> A good comment answers at least one of these questions:
> **What has changed? What is blocking the work? What decision has been made? Or what happens next?**

This reduces repetitive communication and preserves context, especially when several people are working on the same issue at different times.
