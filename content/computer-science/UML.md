Unified Modelling Language (UML)
is a standardised notation 
for representing systems using diagrams. 
In software, class diagrams help to visualise classes, 
interfaces, inheritance, composition and dependencies.

---

A diagram is a model: it should clarify the design, not replicate every detail of the code.
