Root Cause Analysis is about
going deep into why a problem occurred, 
rather than simply solving the problem. 
Understanding the cause as 
a way of understanding the whole system.

---

 In software development, it is a natural part of debugging and incident analysis.

Logs, metrics and `stack` trace help to reconstruct what happened. Techniques such as [[julzdao/computer-science/Five Whys]] can help to dig deeper, provided that the answers are backed up by evidence rather than assumptions.

In the era of [[julzdao/computer-science/Artificial Intelligence|AI]], [[julzdao/computer-science/Root Cause Analysis]] may become one of the most important skills of all. 

> [!warning]
> Root cause analysis should rarely turn into a search for someone to blame. The aim is to discover which technical condition, process or decision allowed the problem to arise.
