Visual Thinking uses diagrams, 
drawings and models to transform 
abstract or complex information 
into a visual representation that 
feels easier to understand and comprehend.

---

It is particularly useful when a problem involves many relationships or states.

```text
Problem → Elements → Relationships → Model → Insight
```

A diagram can show structure and flow; a visual metaphor can help build mental models. Tools such as Excalidraw allow you to do this quickly during research, debugging or technical discussions.

> [!tip]
> If you’ve been trying to explain a problem in words for too long, try drawing it. You don’t need a nice drawing: you need the relationships to be visible.

The sketch can also become a tool for [[julzdao/computer-science/Communication]] to help the team share a common understanding of the problem.
