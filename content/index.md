Welcome to my corner of the digital Universe. 

I'm a software developer exploring how computer science and technology can help build reliable, resource-efficient agricultural systems. Connecting bytes with nature. 

This is not a traditional blog. It is a growing collection of [[Linking your thinking|interconnected notes]], experiments, projects and [Research](./research/). Some ideas are just seeds, others might become evergreen.

## How to explore the garden

1. **Search**: Use the `search` button on the top-left to find any concept, fundamental or insight within the site. Alternatively, press `Cmd + K`  (`ctrl + K` on Windows).
2. **Follow Links**: Navigate to a note and use the inner links, backlinks or tags to dive deeper into the interconnected ideas. 
3. **Graph View**: Interact with the graph view at the bottom of each note to find other connected notes or tags. 

> [!tip] Don't know where to start?
> Select a topic or idea that interests you and follow the links. There is no required reading order, feel free to explore as you wish.
